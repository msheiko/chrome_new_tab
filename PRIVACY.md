# Privacy Policy

**Custom Start Page** (Chrome extension)

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

## Summary

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the extension does

It replaces Chrome's New Tab page with a local page showing a grid of site shortcuts. The list of sites is bundled with the extension.

## Data collection

- No personal or usage data is collected.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used.
- No cookies are set and no data is sent to any server by the extension.
- The extension requests no browser permissions.
- All code runs locally; no remote code is loaded.

## Third parties

The extension does not share data with third parties. Clicking a site shortcut opens that site in your browser, and the site's own privacy policy applies from that point.
